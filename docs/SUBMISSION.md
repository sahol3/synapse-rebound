**Name:** SYNAPSE // REBOUND

**Description (voting page):**

You are Vincent, a courier in Neo-Dhaka who was shot off a rooftop sixty seconds ago. Your implant threw you back in time, your weapons are gone, and all you have left is a wrist-guard that sends enemy fire straight back at whoever fired it. Your handler Saif is on the radio telling you to stay down, while a second voice in your head tells you to climb. The gunship that shot you has learned how you move, and it shows you where it thinks you will be next, so you win by doing something it did not expect. The game has four stages, a boss you beat with its own shots, and three endings.

## itch.io page

**Tagline:** They shot you off the roof. Send it back.

**Description:**

Sixty seconds ago someone shot Vincent off the top of Tower 09. His implant rewound him to the alley below with no weapons and one working tool, a kinetic wrist-guard that bounces enemy fire back where it came from.

You climb back to the roof through four stages. Every shot aimed at you is ammunition, and a shot you return to a shielded enemy comes back faster each time until one of you misses.

Your handler Saif talks to you on the radio the whole way up, and you will hear his voice. A second voice, the Echo, thinks he is lying to you.

The gunship on the roof was trained on how you move. A faint outline shows where it expects you to be, and a number on screen shows how often it has been right about you. Break your own habits and the number falls.

The game also adjusts to how you play, so a first-time player and an expert can both reach the roof. There are three endings, and each one shows what happened to everyone afterwards.

**Controls:**

- A and D to move, Space to jump (hold for height, press again in the air to dash up)
- Mouse to aim, click to deflect toward the cursor
- Shift to air-dash toward the cursor
- W and S to change depth lane right after a deflection
- Q or E to stabilize or overclock a module
- R to rewind to the last checkpoint, Esc to pause, F11 for fullscreen
